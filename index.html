Campus Policy

Hostel Laundry Guidelines

Please follow these shared hygiene and operational rules to keep the laundry service smooth for all hostel residents.

10-Minute Punctuality Rule

Arrive within 10 minutes of your booked slot. If unused after 15 minutes, the slot is automatically released to walk-in students.

Fair Usage Limit

Students can have up to 2 active reservations per week to ensure every hostel wing resident gets access during peak hours.

Liquid Detergent Only

Front-load washers require HE low-suds liquid detergent. Avoid harsh powders that clog the drainage pipes and filters.

Prompt Collection

Empty your clean laundry into your basket promptly at cycle completion so the next student can load their garments on time.
